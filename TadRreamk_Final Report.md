## 1. Executive summary of the progress

Building on the exponential growth reported in the 2024-25 final report, TadReamk Limited has, during the 2025-26 financial year, consolidated its position as Hong Kong's leading AI trademark start-up and converted earlier R&D investment into tangible market traction.

The 2025-26 period has been characterised by five themes:

1. **From single products to a unified software suite.** Whereas in earlier phases TadReamk Eye (TE), TadReamk Create (TC) and MallBuddy were marketed largely as separate offerings, during this reporting period we have packaged them as a coherent **TadReamk software suite** and made our first commercial sales on that basis. In October 2025, the company concluded two sales of the software suite at **RMB 2,000 each** — not merely TC-specific deals, but full-suite engagements covering TadReamk Lace, TadReamk Eye and TadReamk Create. This validates our long-standing strategy of building complementary tools around a shared logo-detection and trademark-similarity backbone.
2. **Re-engineering on top of the new generation of LLMs.** The entire TadReamk software suite was revamped during 2025-26 to adopt new AI coding standards, allowing us to take advantage of the latest, far more capable class of large language models. This systematic re-engineering keeps the suite at the technological frontier without needing to reinvent the wheel for each new model release.
3. **Brand-building through a flagship community initiative.** We designed and ran a **logo design competition** powered by TC. The competition will be carried out in partnership with the Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU) Science Faculty, and the winning concept will be incorporated into the Faculty's **new logo**. This is a highly visible endorsement of TC's capability from a major academic institution and is expected to be a strong reference point for future Government, Business and Consumer (G/B/C) market engagements.
4. **AI-driven marketing as the natural extension of our logo technology.** During this milestone period, we ran a substantial volume of **AI-based marketing**, using AI to auto-generate **videos, blogs and stories** that showcase our reputation in branding. This is the most natural extension of our logo and trademark AI technology: the same generative and recognition capabilities that power TC and TE can be redirected to produce compelling brand storytelling at scale, and we have used this to build TadReamk's own brand presence as well as to demonstrate the storytelling potential of our suite to prospective Business and Consumer customers.
5. **Internal AI tooling for the administrative load of running a start-up.** Towards the end of this milestone period, we mapped out our own internal operations and discovered that the company has to undertake **over 62 distinct administrative tasks** on an ongoing basis. A substantial share of founder and staff time — and therefore a substantial share of funding — was being consumed by paperwork and administration rather than by innovation. In response, we built, for our own company, a set of **state-of-the-art AI tools** that automate these administrative tasks using the latest LLM-based methodologies. These tools are already in active internal use; we now treat them as a **second product line** that, in addition to the core trademark AI technology, we intend to refine, productise and roll out on an ongoing basis going forward.


At the same time, the three-market strategy first articulated last year — **Government (G), Business (B), Consumer (C)** — remains our north star. During 2025-26 we continued to:

- After realising that we had missed the opportunity to pitch directly to the Hong Kong Intellectual Property Department (HKIPD) tender (G market), we began to explore collaborative arrangements with Clarivate — facilitated by the **HKBU Knowledge Transfer Office (KTO)** — and with other Hong Kong-based consultancy partners, with the aim of securing a route into future HKIPD engagements.
- Deepen engagement with cross-border e-commerce companies, particularly Shenzhen-based exporters to Amazon (B market).
- Maintain TE's freemium availability to retain mind-share and reputation within the patent attorney / IP community (C market).

A new strategic addition in 2025-26 is the launch of **"Rachel"**, our chatbot for the trademark AI technology stack defined in our three-year TSSSU proposal. Rachel was subsequently extended into a more general-purpose chatbot capable of interacting with the company's full information base — and, in time, capable of taking over the company's entire human-operated workflows through AI automation. This positions us well not only for the planned AI-assisted trademark filing system, but also for the broader goal of running an AI-native operation.

The 2025-26 period also delivered two significant external milestones that strengthen TadReamk's standing in the wider innovation ecosystem:

- In **September 2025**, TadReamk was admitted to the **HKAI Lab incubation programme**, expanding the company's footprint in Hong Kong's AI ecosystem alongside the ongoing HKSTP Incu-App support. Benefits include **additional Alibaba Cloud credits** and **additional HKSTP workspace**.
- On **11–13 June 2025**, representing **HKBU**, TadReamk **won a Bronze Medal at the 8th China (Shanghai) International Invention and Innovation Exhibition** — the company's first major international award in this reporting period.

The remainder of this report details progress under each required heading.

## 6. Difficulties encountered

The principal difficulties identified in the 2024-25 final report — particularly the **pay-first-and-reimburse-later** model of many government schemes (Talent Hub, STEM internship, etc.), **limited credit-card / banking facilities** for professor-founded tech start-ups, and the **prohibition on double-dipping** between TSSSU/HKSTP funding and other grant programmes such as the Patent Application Grant — have continued to apply cash-flow pressure during 2025-26.

In addition, during the 2025-26 reporting period:

- **Pace of LLM evolution.** While the new generation of LLMs has been transformative for the suite (see Section 2), keeping pace with each new model release has required a non-trivial re-engineering effort to maintain the new AI coding standards across TE, TC, MallBuddy and Rachel.
- **Sales cycle length.** The first suite-level sales (October 2025) confirmed that suite-based selling generates higher-value deals, but at the cost of longer enterprise sales cycles than the original B2C/patent-attorney plan.
- **Missed TSSSU+ opportunity and founder-funded cash flow.** The company was unable to obtain **TSSSU+** funding during the reporting period because it could not secure a qualifying private investor — a prerequisite for the enhanced scheme. Without that additional government support, cash-flow pressure intensified further. The founder has therefore had to inject a substantial amount of **personal capital** into the business (cumulative personal investment now exceeds **HK$1.2 million**) to keep operations and R&D on track while the company continues to pursue commercial revenue and investor engagement.

The first two difficulties above remain "good problems to have" in the sense that they arise primarily because the company is scaling and selling at higher contract values. The **TSSSU+** and founder-funded cash-flow situation, by contrast, reflects a structural funding gap that the company is actively working to close through commercial sales and investor outreach.

## 11. Social and/or community impacts of the technology start-up's R&D work

The original social-impact narrative articulated in the 2023-24 and 2024-25 final reports continues to apply, and is updated below with 2025-26-specific activity.

**Impact for small and medium enterprises (SMEs).** TadReamk's mission of giving SMEs affordable, end-to-end access to trademark technology — from registration through to infringement-risk mitigation — has been reinforced this year by maintaining a freemium consumer tier for TE, ensuring that individual businesses and patent attorneys are not priced out of pre-filing checks even as we monetise higher-value Government and Business engagements.

**Educational and community engagement — HKBU Science Faculty logo competition.** The TC-powered logo competition is a direct community impact: it engages students and the broader HKBU community in a creative, AI-assisted design exercise, with the winning entry being incorporated into the Faculty's new logo. This both showcases what AI-assisted design can do for non-commercial audiences and creates a long-lived visible artefact of the collaboration.

**Sharing the entrepreneurial experience.** Prof. Xu's continued engagement with his community of tens of thousands of machine-learning followers across multiple social media channels — and his presentations during trips to Australia — continue to position TadReamk, HKBU and Hong Kong's start-up ecosystem (including HKSTP) on the international map.

**STEM and youth talent development.** TadReamk and its founder are deeply committed to nurturing the next generation of young talent — a commitment that runs throughout the year and is documented in Section 4. Prof. Xu believes that giving young people hands-on experience in the day-to-day running of a start-up is itself a form of knowledge dissemination, vital for their personal and professional growth. During 2025-26 the company hosted approximately **16 interns and short-term contributors** across the **HKSAR Summer STEM Internship Scheme**, the **HKSTP Youth Internship Programme (YSIP)**, the **PolyU School of Design Internship Programme**, and directly funded roles, drawing from HKBU, HKUST, CUHK, PolyU and other Hong Kong universities. Mr. Chen Chun Hang's earlier progression from summer STEM intern → part-time Research Associate → Researcher → full-time hire remains the flagship example of the long-term career impact of this work.

**Media coverage and exhibitions.** A headline event of the year was TadReamk **representing HKBU at the 8th China (Shanghai) International Invention and Innovation Exhibition (11–13 June 2025)**, where the company **won a Bronze Medal** — recognising both the technical merit of our trademark AI suite and the global relevance of the underlying research. The company's admission to the **HKAI Lab incubation programme in September 2025** has further increased media and ecosystem visibility within Hong Kong. In addition, **Prof. Xu and TadReamk Limited featured on the cover page of *Ta Kung Pao***, one of Hong Kong's leading Chinese-language newspapers — a significant public endorsement of the company's work and its contribution to Hong Kong's innovation ecosystem.
